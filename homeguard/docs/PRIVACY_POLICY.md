# HomeGuard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

HomeGuard is a client app for security equipment (NVRs, IP cameras and IP intercoms) that **you own and operate**.

## Data we collect
**None.** HomeGuard has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer never receives any data from the app.

## Data stored on your device
- **Device credentials and settings.** Usernames, passwords, IP addresses and preferences are kept in the operating system's secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). They are never included in cloud backups.
- **Snapshots, clips and event history** that you capture or import are stored in the app's private storage.

You can delete all of this at any time with *Settings → Erase all data on this device*, or by uninstalling the app.

## Network connections
The app connects **only** to the device addresses you enter yourself:
- on your local network, or
- over a remote route you configure (your VPN, or TLS-protected addresses).

Video, audio and door-relay commands travel directly between your phone and your equipment.

## Permissions
- **Microphone**: only while you use two-way talk with your door station.
- **Biometrics**: optional unlock and door-unlock confirmation. Biometric data never leaves the OS; the app only receives a yes/no result.
- **Network / Wi-Fi multicast**: to reach and discover your devices on the local network.

## Children
HomeGuard is not directed at children.
